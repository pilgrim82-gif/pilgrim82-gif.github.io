Terms of Service

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Short Video Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner. It sends videos the owner created to the owner's own TikTok accounts as drafts, using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Use

The tool is not offered to the public. Only TikTok accounts that the owner controls are connected. The owner reviews and publishes every draft inside the TikTok app.

Content

All videos are created by the owner and follow TikTok's Community Guidelines and Developer Terms of Service.

Disconnecting

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, without warranty of any kind.
