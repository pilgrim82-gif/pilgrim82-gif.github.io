Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Short Video Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner. It does not collect, sell, or share personal information of any other person.

What the tool accesses

With the owner's authorization, the tool reads the display name of the connected TikTok account (scope user.info.basic) and uploads videos to that account's drafts (scope video.upload).

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on a server and no data is sent to third parties other than TikTok.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.
